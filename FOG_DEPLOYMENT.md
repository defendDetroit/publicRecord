## FOG RULES

1. **Each post stands alone.** Nobody needs to read the whole site. Each post is self-contained with ONE insight.
2. **Link to the specific article, not the homepage.** Deep links. The fog is everywhere at once.
3. **Match the community's tone.** r/netsec wants technical rigor. r/IndianCountry wants respect. r/gaming wants the Skyrim hook. Same underlying work, different surface.
4. **Don't cross-promote.** Never say "also see my post on r/geology." The fog doesn't point to itself. People find the connections organically.
5. **Let the membrane validate.** After each post, watch the membrane for new reader patterns. The fog tells you where it landed.
6. **The social posts (Twitter, Mastodon, Bluesky, LinkedIn) fire on Day 1.** These are the humidity before the fog.
