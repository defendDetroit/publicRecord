## The February 28, 2022 Decision

### What the Law Required
- **MCL 388.1763(2)**: District employing uncredentialed individual → 50% state aid deduction of salary paid
- **MCL 388.1763(6)**: School official **knowingly** continuing employment after MDE notification → **misdemeanor, $1,500 fine per incident**
- **MCL 380.1246**: Superintendent must hold valid Michigan School Administrator credential

### What Her Office Did
| Time | Action | Actor |
|------|--------|-------|
| Feb 22, 12:24 PM | Leah Breen (Schmiedeknecht's director) warns MacDowell that Banks has no credential and recommends pausing employment | Leah Breen (Director, OEE) |
| Feb 28, 8:26 AM | Schmiedeknecht sends formal investigation letter to Wells-Stallworth | **Schmiedeknecht** |
| Feb 28, ~8:40 AM | Wells-Stallworth responds — retains attorney Gregory Meihn within 14 minutes | Nicole Wells-Stallworth |
| Feb 28, 9:13 AM | Attorney Meihn: "I have not fix for this at this time" | Gregory M. Meihn |
| Feb 28, **10:50 AM** | **Schmiedeknecht gives the loophole: "As long as Mr. Banks is not working in a position requiring a valid credential until his School Administrator permit is issued, there will be no need for an investigation."** | **Schmiedeknecht** |
| Feb 28, 10:50 AM | **Schmiedeknecht: "Please disregard the survey that will be sent to you on Friday morning."** | **Schmiedeknecht** |

### What Should Have Happened
1. Under MCL 388.1763(2), MDE should have assessed a **state aid deduction** for the period Banks worked without a credential
2. Under MCL 388.1763(6), if Wells-Stallworth **knowingly continued** employing Banks after notification, she should have been charged with a **misdemeanor**
3. The investigation survey (Qualtrics) should have been completed, not disregarded
4. OEE should have followed up to verify compliance — not taken the school's word for it
