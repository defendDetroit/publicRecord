## ⚡ Mandatory Closure Trigger — MCL 380.507

The state superintendent can **suspend DPSCD's authority to issue new charter contracts** if DPSCD is not providing appropriate continuing oversight. DPSCD authorized PCA under the same operator whose existing school is in the bottom 3%.
