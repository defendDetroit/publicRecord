## How This Page Works

1. **Default assumption:** Good faith. Everyone starts as "wronged" unless evidence shows otherwise.
2. **Reclassification:** If evidence emerges that a listed party was an active participant, this page is updated with the evidence and the party is reclassified. All changes are tracked in git history — the original good-faith assumption and the reason for reclassification are permanently visible.
3. **Self-sorting:** Institutions and individuals who see their names here have a choice — investigate and distance themselves, or stay silent. Their response (or lack thereof) becomes part of the record.
4. **Open invitation:** Anyone listed here who believes they were wronged is invited to contribute their experience. Your account may corroborate the pattern documented elsewhere in this repository.
