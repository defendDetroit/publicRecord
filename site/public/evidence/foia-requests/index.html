What Was Received

David Head proactively sent the TCR 22-12 Final Decision and Order — the administrator certificate hearing decision — along with a cover letter. This was not part of the original FOIA request; the coordinator sent it voluntarily.

TCR 22-12 — Final Decision and Order (August 4, 2023)

State Superintendent Michael F. Rice issued Banks’s administrator certificate with seven (7) restrictive conditions:

ConditionRequirement
#1Background check every 3 years
#2Report criminal charges to MDE within 3 business days
#3Shall not handle cash
#4Shall not be the sole person to approve expenditures
#5Complete 6 credit hours professional development annually
#6Submit to compliance verification every 6 months
#7Board verifies compliance every 6 months (signed by Board Chair)

Key details the FOIA revealed:

  • The Department of Education objected to reinstatement
  • The Attorney General argued against the certificate
  • The Administrative Law Judge recommended no conditions (rubber stamp)
  • Superintendent Rice overruled the ALJ and added the 7 conditions
  • The compliance verifications under Condition #7 are signed by Nicole Wells-Stallworth — MacDowell Board Chair and former CEO of The Children’s Center of Wayne County

The note that matters: David Head signaled that compliance verification documents exist but were not part of the original request. This prompted FOIA Request #2.

FOIA Request #2 — MDE (October 1, 2026)

FieldDetail
AgencyMichigan Department of Education
Date SentOctober 1, 2026
Due DateOctober 8, 2026
CoordinatorDavid Head
Status⏳ Pending — awaiting response

What Was Requested

  1. Condition #7 compliance verifications — the documents signed by Wells-Stallworth every six months attesting to Banks’s compliance
  2. Board correspondence related to Banks’s certificate
  3. The Management Agreement between Purpose Group LLC and PCA (the document containing the false §5.2 “No Related Parties” representation)
  4. DPSCD authorization records for Purpose Charter Academy

Why This Matters

The Condition #7 verifications are signed by the same person (Wells-Stallworth) who:

  • Served as CEO of The Children’s Center of Wayne County
  • Serves as Board Chair of MacDowell Preparatory Academy
  • Signs off on compliance for a man with 8 felony convictions

If the verifications attest that Banks is complying with Condition #4 (“shall not be the sole person to approve expenditures”), but Banks is the sole member of Purpose Group LLC through which 72.67% of revenue flows — the verifications may be inaccurate.
