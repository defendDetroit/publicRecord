What to Look For

When evaluating any judicial candidate, check:

  1. Board memberships — Is the judge on the board of any entity connected to litigants? (Miller: Board Chair of Banks’ foundation)
  2. Campaign contributions — Who funded the campaign? (Banks Strategy LLC → Miller)
  3. Family connections — Do family members have business relationships with litigants? (Miller’s husband’s arrest in Banks’ vehicle)
  4. Professional overlap — Did the judge attend school with, work with, or serve alongside litigants? (Baker College → Miller + Banks)

Verify It Yourself

Every claim above can be checked using free, public databases:

  • Campaign finance: Michigan CFRS — search “Cylenthia Miller” or “Banks Strategy”
  • Business filings: LARA MiBusiness Registry — search “Anchor Rock Foundation”
  • Criminal records: Michigan OTIS — search “Brian Banks”
  • Court records: Wayne County Courts — case search
