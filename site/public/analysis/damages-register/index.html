Tier 1: Judges Who Crossed the Membrane

Judges hold the highest public trust. When a judge uses or fails to use judicial authority while connected to the enterprise, the damage is structural — it undermines the legitimacy of every order from that bench.

Judge Cylenthia Miller

Court: Wayne County 3rd Circuit Court Public Position: Elected Judge The Act: Board Chair of Anchor Rock Foundation — a Brian Banks entity — while sitting as an active judge. A judge on the board of a convicted felon’s nonprofit. [Record: LARA filing, Anchor Rock Foundation] Legal Theory: Judicial misconduct under MCR 9.104(1)–(4); 42 USC § 1983 (deprivation of rights under color of law if she ruled on matters touching the enterprise) Damage: Every litigant in her courtroom is entitled to ask whether they got a judge or a board member.

Court: 36th District Court Public Position: Elected Judge The Act: Campaign’s sole reported expenditure was $383.82 to Banks Strategy & Consultants LLC. The only vendor a judge’s campaign paid was the consulting firm of a man with 9 convictions. [Record: Michigan CFRS, Yancey campaign filing] Legal Theory: MCR 9.205 (judicial campaign conduct); appearance of impropriety under MCR 2.003(C)(1) Damage: Banks Strategy received judicial campaign money. The same Banks Strategy run by a 6-time felon.
