What Is An Operating System?

In computing, an operating system mediates between hardware and applications. It allocates resources, manages processes, and controls access. A healthy OS distributes resources to producers. A parasitic OS extracts resources for intermediaries.

This study maps a parasitic operating system in Michigan politics using entirely public records. The model organism is Dykema Gossett PLLC — a national law firm that doesn’t just provide legal services to dark money nonprofits. It creates them, staffs them, and operates them.

Every claim below can be verified from the public sources cited.

The Kernel: W. Alan Wilk

Every OS has a kernel — the privileged code that controls everything.

W. Alan Wilk is a Member at Dykema Gossett and Team Leader of its Political Compliance, Elections & Campaign Finance practice. He is admitted to the Michigan bar (1995), educated at James Madison College (MSU) and Boston College Law.

He incorporates dark money nonprofits. He files their state paperwork. He serves as their lawyer. He manages their compliance. He is also a member of the National Republican Lawyers Association — and simultaneously the lawyer for the Democratic mayor’s campaign.

When asked for comment by Deadline Detroit, Wilk said: “I’m not authorized to speak to the press on this.”

The kernel doesn’t speak to userspace.

Source: dykema.com · Deadline Detroit

The simplest proof that SDJ is not independent:

The phone number on Save Detroit Jobs’ IRS 990EZ filing (EIN 81-3772058) is 517-374-9100.

That is Dykema Gossett’s Lansing office phone number.

If you call Save Detroit Jobs, you reach Dykema’s receptionist.

The entity’s tax return was prepared by Olivia RC A Flower at Dykema Gossett PLLC, 2723 S State St Ste 400, Ann Arbor MI 48104 — Dykema’s Ann Arbor office.

SDJ doesn’t have its own phone. Doesn’t have its own office. Doesn’t prepare its own taxes. Its compliance officers are Dykema employees. Its incorporation was filed by a Dykema attorney.

SDJ is not a nonprofit that uses Dykema. SDJ is a Dykema process wearing a 501(c)(4) costume.
