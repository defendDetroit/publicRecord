From Localization to Permeability

The Anderson Localization analysis showed how disorder in the judicial lattice traps cases — like electrons in a disordered crystal, cases entering captured courts cannot propagate to fair hearings.

This page documents a second phenomenon from the same physics: Anderson permeability — when the membranes between institutions dissolve entirely.

In condensed matter physics, a membrane’s permeability determines what passes through it. A healthy cell membrane is selectively permeable: nutrients in, waste out, toxins blocked. When the membrane fails — when everything passes through — the cell dies.

Institutions have membranes too:

MembraneWhat It Should BlockWhat It Should Allow
CredentialUnqualified staffCredentialed educators
AuthorizationAuthorizer staff at authorized schoolIndependent oversight
GovernanceConflicts of interestIndependent board members
DynastyFamily concentration in one enterpriseMerit-based hiring
EnforcementContinued operation after findingsInvestigation → correction
TransparencyHidden staff, unlisted employeesPublic accountability

Anderson Localization + Anderson Permeability

The two Anderson phenomena work together:

PhenomenonDomainEffect
LocalizationCourtsCases trapped at captured benches — cannot propagate to fair hearing
PermeabilityInstitutionsMembranes dissolved — no boundary between authorizer and authorized, between family and enterprise, between oversight and operation

Localization traps the output (cases). Permeability removes the input filters (hiring, authorization, oversight). Together, they create a system where unqualified people operate unchecked institutions that generate cases trapped in captured courts.

The children at PCA and MacDowell — 90.9% economically disadvantaged, 99.2% Black — enter a school with no functional membranes, taught by staff with no discoverable credentials, governed by judges with conflicts of interest, authorized by an agency whose employees work at the school. If a parent contests this system, their case enters a court where captured judges sit on the same school boards.

The input has no filter. The output has no exit.
