The Metro Property Group Scheme (per federal complaint)

  • Purchased thousands of Detroit foreclosed homes for $500–$5,000
  • Sold to foreign investors (UK, Yemen, Australia) for $40,000–$50,000
  • Represented homes as “refurbished,” “turnkey,” “Section 8 approved” — they were uninhabitable
  • Forged lease documents for non-existent tenants
  • Fabricated Section 8 approvals and post-dated audits
  • Charged investors for “evictions” of fictional tenants
  • 371-paragraph, 116-page complaint with 13 counts including fraud, RICO, and consumer protection

2017 Auction Bidding

Adam Sabree listed as auction bidder in the 2017 Wayne County tax auction. His father Eric Sabree was the Wayne County Treasurer administering the sale.

Father’s FBI Investigation (2019)

Detroit News (Mar 15, 2019): “The FBI is looking into land deals by the family of Wayne County Treasurer Eric Sabree.” FBI Special Agent Mara Schneider “neither confirmed nor denied the existence of an investigation.” County rules banned family members from bidding at tax auctions — violation confirmed by Detroit News reporting. Ethics complaint filed by County Exec Warren Evans — dismissed 5–1 (Jul 2019) on technicality. FBI investigation outcome never publicly reported. No indictment. No exoneration.
