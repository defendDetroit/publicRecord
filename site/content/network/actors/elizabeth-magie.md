## Elizabeth Magie

**Creator** | **Tier 5** | **1 connections**

1866-1948. Created The Landlord's Game (1903) to teach the evils of monopolies. Charles Darrow copied it, sold it to Parker Brothers, became a millionaire. Magie got $500 and no credit. The game about stealing was itself stolen. She also patented improvements to typewriters and was a stenographer, actress, and Georgist activist.
