## Judicial Tenure Commission

**Type:** State oversight body | **Authority:** Michigan Constitution Art. 6 §30 | **Purpose:** Investigate judicial misconduct

**Address:** 3034 W. Grand Blvd., Suite 8-450, Detroit, Michigan 48202

---

## Role in the Referral Loop

The JTC is where AGC sends complaints about judges. The AGC redirected File 26-2141 (complaint about Judge Miller) to JTC on October 9, 2026.

**The problem:** JTC investigations are **confidential** until formal charges are filed. Public accountability is invisible until the commission decides to act publicly.
