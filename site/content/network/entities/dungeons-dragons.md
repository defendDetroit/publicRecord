## Dungeons & Dragons

**Commons** | **Tier 5** | **4 connections**

1974. Gary Gygax and Dave Arneson, Lake Geneva WI. The first tabletop RPG. Collaborative storytelling, probability (dice), creative problem-solving, literacy (you have to READ the rulebook). Spawned an entire genre. Now owned by Hasbro/Wizards of the Coast. 50M+ players.
