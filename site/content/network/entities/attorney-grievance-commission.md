## Self-Initiation Authority

MCR 9.109 gives the Grievance Administrator the power to **self-initiate** investigations. From the AGC's own process document:

> "The Grievance Administrator can initiate a request for investigation in his own name as well."

Goetz can verify every claim in under 60 seconds:

- State Bar: Banks has no JD ✔
- ICHAT: Banks has 9 convictions ✔
