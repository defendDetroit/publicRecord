Judge Miller served as Board Chair of Banks' Anchor Rock Foundation **while serving on the Wayne County bench**. The same judge is under [JTC complaint](/network/judges/cylenthia-miller/) for ethical violations and is seeking re-election **November 3, 2026**.

Independent corroboration: [Bryant v. Miller](/analysis/allied-cases/) (2:26-cv-10917) — a separate litigant also identified Miller as a problem.

## Significance

A sitting judge chairing a convicted felon's nonprofit entity while that felon operates charter schools feeding cases into the same county court system is a textbook judicial ethics violation. The Anchor Rock Foundation connection is one of three documented Miller–Banks ties (along with [Purpose Charter Academy](/network/entities/purpose-charter-academy/) board service and Baker College teaching overlap).
