## Charter Authorization Role {{ confidence(level="verified") }}

Vaughn sat on the DPSCD board throughout the period when PCA was authorized. She was **Board President** when the most recent charter renewal/authorization decisions were made.

DPSCD is the **charter authorizer** for:
- **Purpose Charter Academy** (Banks' school — Judge Miller is Board Chair)
- **MacDowell Preparatory Academy** (Banks' school — Judge Yancey is Board Chair)

The DPSCD earns up to **3% of per-pupil funding** (~$113K/yr) for authorizing these charters — creating a financial incentive to continue authorization.
