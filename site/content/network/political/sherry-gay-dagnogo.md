## Key Facts

- Held a Detroit-area legislative seat in the Michigan House (HD-8)
- Served on the DPSCD board — the entity that **authorized** PCA and MacDowell to operate
- **Resigned from DPSCD in October 2025** after City Council selected her as Detroit's next ombudsman (6–3 vote, Sep 30, 2025)
- Was publicly honored by Banks at his Congressional Black Caucus party
- Direct conflict while on board: a member with social/political ties to a charter operator she oversaw
- Seat filled by Bessie Harris (Oct 2025)

## Ombudsman Appointment (October 2025)

Detroit City Council appointed Gay-Dagnogo ombudsman on **September 30, 2025**. Her 10-year term began **October 6, 2025**. The ombudsman investigates citizen complaints and serves as liaison between residents and city government.

She resigned from the DPSCD board because the city charter prohibits holding another compensated office of trust or profit while serving as ombudsman.
