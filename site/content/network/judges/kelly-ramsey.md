### Alexis Ramsey — The Gateway

Alexis Ramsey was **President of the Highland Park School Board** when she:

1. Pushed to replace Leona Group (for-profit charter operator) with Promise Schools (2019)
2. **"Strongly suggested on more than one occasion"** that Promise Schools hire [Brian Banks](/network/actors/brian-banks/) (per CEO Melissa Hamann to board member Eban Morales)
3. Promise Schools then managed **MacDowell Prep** — giving Banks institutional access to the school he would later take over
4. Ramsey claimed she "merely mentioned" Banks; Morales says Hamann told him Ramsey "strongly suggested" it
5. **DPSCD rejected Banks** during the same period — Asst. Supt. Chrystal Wilson: "more detailed information regarding Mr. Banks's background was realized that made it untenable"
6. Promise Schools CEO Hamann said she **"would consider hiring Banks again"**

Alexis Ramsey also appeared on Todd Perkins' podcast "Perkins for the People" (Mar 2026). Perkins was City Attorney for Highland Park where Alexis was school board president.
