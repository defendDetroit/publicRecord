## Father's FBI Investigation (2019)

Detroit News (Mar 15, 2019): "The FBI is looking into land deals by the family of Wayne County Treasurer Eric Sabree." FBI Special Agent Mara Schneider "neither confirmed nor denied the existence of an investigation."

- **U.S. Development Services LLC** (formed 2002) — wife **Badriyyah** bought 3 Harper Woods homes at 2011 tax auction while Sabree was deputy treasurer coordinating the sale
- County rules **banned** family members from bidding — violation confirmed by Detroit News
- 10 family properties owed **$29,000 in delinquent taxes** — paid off **10 days** after Detroit News inquired
- Son Yusuf Sabree lives in one of the Harper Woods homes; son [Adam Sabree](/network/judges/adam-sabree/) listed as auction bidder in 2017
- Ethics complaint filed by County Exec Warren Evans — **dismissed 5–1** (Jul 2019) on technicality
- **FBI investigation outcome never publicly reported. No indictment. No exoneration.**
