## "Revelation Day" Theory {{ confidence(level="unconfirmed", note="strong circumstantial fit but not confirmed against court records") }}

Banks' book describes a **"Revelation Day"** — a female Oakland County Circuit Court judge at his **1999 felony sentencing** for credit card fraud who "saw greatness in him."

**Analysis (not confirmed):** Denise Langford Morris served on Oakland County Circuit Court from 1992–2022. As the **first female African American judge** on that court, she is a strong candidate for the judge Banks describes. **This identification has not been independently confirmed** against court records or ICHAT data in the source material.

**If confirmed as the "Revelation Day" judge:** She sentenced Banks in 1999 for credit card fraud, then decades later wrote the foreword endorsing his fraudulent "J.D." credential — a credential she would have known was impossible because she sentenced the man who would later claim it.
