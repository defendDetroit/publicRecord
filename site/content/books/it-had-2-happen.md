## The Book

*It Had 2 Happen* (ISBN 173575403X) is Brian Banks' self-published autobiography. The Amazon listing credits the author as **"Brian Banks J.D."** — a credential the State Bar of Michigan cannot confirm. {{ confidence(level="documented") }}

The book's central thesis is that Banks' criminal history was a necessary precursor to his current role as an education leader. The title itself — *It Had 2 Happen* — frames 9 criminal convictions not as choices with consequences, but as predetermined steps in a redemption narrative.
