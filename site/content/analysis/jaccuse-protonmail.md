## The Demarcation

There is a razor-sharp line between two things that look identical from the outside:

| | **Privacy** | **Secrecy** |
|---|---|---|
| **Who it protects** | Individuals FROM institutions | Institutions FROM individuals |
| **Who it serves** | The vulnerable | The powerful |
| **What it hides** | Personal life from surveillance | Wrongdoing from accountability |
| **When it's used** | Always legitimate | Legitimate only when transparent |

**ProtonMail markets privacy.** Protect your emails from Google. Protect your communications from governments. Protect your identity from corporations.

**ProtonMail delivered secrecy.** Protected the investigated from the investigator. Protected the silent agencies from the FOIA requests. Protected the pattern from the person trying to document it.

**The demarcation:** when the tool protects the individual from the institution, it is privacy. When the tool protects the institution from the individual, it is secrecy. ProtonMail crossed the line. Not by intention. By mechanism. But crossing is crossing.
