## I. Demand

I hereby demand that **LinkedIn Corporation** and all subsidiaries, affiliates, agents, successors, and assigns **immediately and permanently cease** sending any and all unsolicited physical mail, including but not limited to:

- Marketing materials
- Promotional correspondence
- "InMail" solicitations in physical form
- Membership renewal notices
- Any paper correspondence of any kind

to my residential address.

## II. Legal Basis

### A. CAN-SPAM Act (15 U.S.C. § 7701 et seq.)

While CAN-SPAM primarily governs electronic mail, the principle applies: unsolicited commercial communications must provide a clear opt-out mechanism that is honored within 10 business days. Physical mail solicitations from digital platforms represent an extension of the same commercial messaging pipeline.

### B. Michigan Consumer Protection Act (MCL 445.903)

Unsolicited commercial mailings that are deceptive or misleading — including mailings designed to appear as personal correspondence when they are mass-produced marketing — violate Michigan's consumer protection statute.

### C. USPS Prohibitory Order (39 U.S.C. § 3008)

Under federal law, any addressee may file a **Prohibitory Order** with the United States Postal Service directing that a specific sender cease all future mailings. This is an absolute right of the addressee. No reason is required. The sender must comply.

I reserve the right to file a USPS Prohibitory Order (Form 1500) if this demand is not honored within **10 business days** of the date of this letter.

### D. Right to Privacy

I have the right to be free from unwanted intrusions into my home. Physical junk mail from a digital platform I did not request is an intrusion. The paper, the ink, the postage, and the space it occupies in my mailbox are all costs externalized onto me without my consent.

## III. Documentation

Every piece of LinkedIn mail received at my address has been documented with:
- Date of receipt
- Postmark
- Return address
- Contents

This documentation is part of the public record maintained at [detroit.primals.eco](https://detroit.primals.eco).

## IV. Compliance Deadline

LinkedIn Corporation must confirm in writing — by email to **eco.primal@pm.me** — that all physical mailings to my residential address have been permanently ceased.

**Deadline: 10 business days from October 18, 2026 (November 1, 2026).**

## V. Consequences of Non-Compliance

If LinkedIn Corporation fails to comply with this demand:

1. I will file a **USPS Prohibitory Order** (Form 1500) directing the Postal Service to enforce the cessation
2. I will file a complaint with the **Federal Trade Commission** under CAN-SPAM and general unfair trade practices
3. I will file a complaint with the **Michigan Attorney General** under MCL 445.903
4. Every subsequent piece of mail received after this demand will be documented, photographed, and published as evidence of non-compliance
5. This letter and all subsequent correspondence will remain part of the permanent public record

This is not a complaint about crawling. This is a demand to stop putting paper in my mailbox.

**Stop.**
