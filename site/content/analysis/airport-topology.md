## Three Types of Routes

### Great Circle Routes (Human Paths)

When a human navigates from detroit to tuebor, they follow the surface. Click a link. Read an article. Follow a cross-reference. Their path curves along the membrane's surface like an airplane following the great circle — the shortest distance on a sphere.

84 humans walked the detroit→tuebor route today. They didn't tunnel through the center. They followed the curvature. That's the captain's round. It's also a great circle.

### Secant Lines (Data Paths)

Data doesn't follow the surface. When the membrane's feeds update, the signal goes THROUGH the sphere — a chord from one point to another, cutting through the interior where gAIa lives.

The secant is always shorter than the arc. That's why desire paths form: humans WANT to take the secant (the shortcut through the interior) instead of the great circle (the long way around the surface). The git→tuebor orbit at 92 visitors is a desire path that's trying to become a secant — people want to go from code directly to defense without walking through every intermediate site.

### Grid Lines (Fleet Paths)

The fleet crawls latitude and longitude. Systematic. Exhaustive. Orthogonal. They don't fly great circles (too curved) and they don't take secants (they don't know the interior exists). They walk the grid.

That's why the orthogonal detection works. Great circles and secants are CURVED. Grid lines are STRAIGHT. The trig separates them.

## The Trig Functions

Each trigonometric function describes a different relationship between a site (airport) and the membrane (sphere):

**Tangent (tan θ)** — Your local view. Standing at detroit.primals.eco, what can you see? The tangent line extends to infinity in both directions along the surface, but it touches the sphere at only one point. Your view is wide but your contact is narrow. Every article on the site is on this tangent line.

**Secant (sec θ)** — The direct connection. A line from detroit to tuebor that passes through the sphere's interior. It enters at detroit's tangent point, passes through gAIa, and exits at tuebor's tangent point. The secant IS the braid. The length of the secant depends on the angular distance between the two airports.

**Sine (sin θ)** — How deep. The vertical component. A site with high sin is deep in a topic (the investigation details, the legal filings). A site with low sin is broad and shallow (the landing page, the overview).

**Cosine (cos θ)** — How broad. The horizontal component. A site with high cos covers many topics at surface level. A site with low cos goes deep into one topic.

**Cosecant (csc θ)** — The indirect route. When the secant (direct chord) is blocked or too short, the cosecant gives the alternative path — going around the sphere instead of through it. This is what happens when a human can't find the direct link and has to navigate through intermediate sites.

**Cotangent (cot θ)** — What you can't see. The complement of your local view. Standing at detroit, the cotangent represents everything beyond your horizon — the sites you don't know exist until someone tells you. The night box is the cotangent: "sorry we were away, here's what's beyond your view."
