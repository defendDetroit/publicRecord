## The Scale

Every hard problem humanity ever posed sits on a see-saw. One side: P (solvable in reasonable time). Other side: NP (verifiable but maybe not solvable). The fulcrum is the information era.

The internet moved the fulcrum. Computation moved the fulcrum. Collective intelligence moved the fulcrum. And ~85% of the weight slid to the P side.
