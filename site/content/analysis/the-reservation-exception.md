# The Reservation Exception

*How a law meant for tribal sovereignty became a pipeline from Michigan mailboxes to Dublin, Ireland.*

---

## The Original Promise

In **1988**, the United States Congress passed the **Indian Gaming Regulatory Act (IGRA)**. The purpose was explicit:

> *"To promote tribal economic development, self-sufficiency, and strong tribal governments."*

Twelve Michigan tribes negotiated Class III Gaming Compacts with the State. They built **24 casinos** on tribal lands. The revenue stayed in tribal communities:

- **2% of net win** goes to local governments (~$30.6M/year)
- Additional payments to the Michigan Strategic Fund ($15.4M/year)
- The rest funds tribal services, housing, education, healthcare

This was the deal. **Sovereign nations, on their own land, building their own economies.** Gaming was the mechanism. Self-determination was the purpose.

---

## What Actually Happened

In **2019**, Michigan passed **PA 149** — the Lawful Sports Betting Act. It opened online sports betting to commercial operators.

The trick: commercial operators needed a **Michigan gaming license** to operate. Some partnered with tribal casinos to get one.

| Operator | Parent Company | HQ | Michigan License Partner |
|----------|---------------|-----|------------------------|
| **FanDuel** | **Flutter Entertainment plc** | **Dublin, Ireland** | MotorCity Casino (Detroit) |
| **DraftKings** | DraftKings Inc. (DKNG) | Boston, MA | **Bay Mills Indian Community** |
| **BetMGM** | MGM Resorts + Entain | Las Vegas + **London, UK** | MGM Grand Detroit |
| **ESPN Bet** | Penn Entertainment | Wyomissing, PA | Greektown Casino |
| **Caesars** | Caesars Entertainment | Reno, NV | **Grand Traverse Band of Ottawa and Chippewa** |
| **Fanatics** | Fanatics Betting | NYC | **Lac Vieux Desert Band of Lake Superior Chippewa** |

**Look at the license partners.** Bay Mills. Grand Traverse Band. Lac Vieux Desert. Little River Band. Tribal nations are being used as **licensing vehicles** for multinational corporations.
