## The Question

Is corruption in Detroit's charter school network **organic** — the product of ordinary self-dealing, opportunistic behavior, and uncoordinated greed? Or is it **crafted** — designed by a competent architect using systematic methods to create defensible, compartmentalized, self-reinforcing structures?

The answer determines everything. Organic corruption decays on its own. Crafted corruption regenerates.

## The Evidence

### 1. Hub-and-Spoke Compartmentalization

Both central actors — the superintendent who runs the schools and the attorney who forms the dark money vehicles — have clustering coefficients of **0.022.** This is identical and near zero.

In a normal social or political network, clustering coefficients range from 0.2 to 0.6. People who know each other tend to know each other's contacts. The network forms clusters — groups where everyone is connected.

A clustering coefficient of 0.022 means: this person's contacts **do not know each other.** The network is a hub-and-spoke architecture — every connection goes through the central node and nowhere else.

This is not how organic corruption works. Organic corruption is messy. People talk. Word gets around. Connections form between co-conspirators naturally.

Hub-and-spoke is how **designed** corruption works. The architect places each contact in a separate spoke. They don't need to know each other. They only need to know the hub. Compartmentalization is a feature, not a bug.

### 2. The Formation-Governance Split

One attorney forms dark money vehicles. A different person governs them. Same employer. Seven entities, same pattern every time. In [Found the Leak](/analysis/the-flower-to-wilk/), we documented eleven vehicles formed by one Dykema Gossett attorney. At every vehicle where both names appear, the first forms and the second governs. The division is absolute.

No natural process produces a formation/governance split across seven entities. This is architectural.

### 3. Single Point of Failure — By Design

Remove the superintendent from the network graph and it shatters into **twelve pieces.** No other node even comes close. He is a **+10 cut vertex** — the only structural element holding the enterprise together. There is no deputy. No backup. No successor.

Remove the formation attorney and **five dark money vehicles** orphan immediately — disconnected from everything else in the graph.

This looks like vulnerability. It is. But it's also **control.** A system with redundancy has distributed power. A system with no redundancy has absolute power — concentrated in the single point. The fragility is the feature. It ensures no one else can operate the machine.

### 4. Two Communities, Three Bridges

When you let the graph self-organize — no labels, no instructions, just the math — it converges to exactly **two communities:**

- **Community 1 (73%):** The enterprise — schools, courts, judges, PACs, political connections, family networks, vendors.
- **Community 2 (20%):** The formation machine — dark money vehicles, the law firm, the compliance team, the political PACs.

They connect through exactly three bridges:
1. A judge who **recused** from a related case (betweenness: 0.3286)
2. A dark money vehicle named **Save Detroit Jobs** (betweenness: 0.3195)
3. The **law firm itself** (betweenness: 0.3580)

Cut any two of those three bridges and the communities separate completely.

The recusal is particularly telling. The judge recused because the connection exists. The recusal IS the signal that the bridge is real. The math confirms what the recusal implied — that judge, with only three connections, is the **third most structurally critical node** in the entire 129-node graph.

### 5. Edge Type Diversity

The superintendent connects to his network through **twenty different relationship types:** employment, family, governance, ownership, payment, political endorsement, campaign vendor relationships, mentorship, corruption, legal representation, credential legitimization, referral, institutional memory, classmate networks, family donations, and more.

No other node has more than ten. This diversity is the resilience. Cut any one type of connection and nineteen others remain.

## The Hypothesis

**If the corruption is crafted rather than organic, the structural patterns will be consistent, repeatable, and distinguishable from noise.**

The artisan's precision is the vulnerability. A sloppy system produces noise. A craftsman produces signal. The regularity that makes the architecture defensible also makes it structurally unique.

If the artisan hypothesis is correct:

1. **Clustering coefficients** will remain near 0.022 as the graph grows. The compartmentalization is maintained deliberately.
2. **The two-community structure** will persist. New nodes will be absorbed into existing communities, not form new ones.
3. **The same three bridges** will remain critical. New bridges will be architectural (law firm connections) not organic (social connections).
4. **Cut vertex fragility** will remain high. The architecture is designed without redundancy.
5. **The formation/governance split** will appear in every new vehicle discovered. The template is reused.
