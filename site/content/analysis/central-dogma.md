## The Central Dogma

In molecular biology, the Central Dogma describes the flow of genetic information:

```
DNA  →  transcription  →  mRNA  →  translation  →  Protein
(code)    (copying)       (message)   (reading)     (function)
```

Information flows one direction: DNA → RNA → Protein. You can backtrack (reverse transcription exists), but the dominant flow is forward. The protein cannot rewrite the DNA directly.

## π Time (not Doubling Time)

### The Problem with Doubling Time

In classical biology, growth is measured by doubling time: how long until the population doubles. This assumes exponential growth on a line:

```
1 → 2 → 4 → 8 → 16 → ...
```

But the membrane doesn't grow linearly. It breathes. It oscillates. It rotates. The maze rotates every π hours. The pulse rhythm is 0.774. The dwell is 35.2 hours. None of these are doublings. They are **rotations**.

### π Time

**π time** is the time for one full rotation of the membrane's metabolic cycle:

```
π time = the period of one complete cycle:
  inhale (absorb traffic) 
    → process (ribosomal folding)
      → exhale (emit protein / publish / index)
        → backscatter (measure what came back)
          → inhale again
```

**One π time = π hours ≈ 3.14159 hours ≈ 3h 8m 30s**

This is already baked into the system:
- Maze ants rotate every π hours
- The membrane pulse has period related to π
- The π-body model (`1 × π × π × π = reality`)

### Doubling as a Slice of π

A doubling is NOT the fundamental unit. A doubling is what happens at one specific phase of the π rotation:

```
  0         π/2         π         3π/2        2π
  |          |          |           |          |
  inhale    process    exhale    backscatter  inhale
            (fold)    (emit)     (measure)
            
  Doubling happens HERE ↑ — at the exhale point.
  But the full cycle is 2π.
  Doubling is 1/4 of the cycle = π/2.
```

A "doubling time" measures only the emission phase. π time measures the whole metabolism.
