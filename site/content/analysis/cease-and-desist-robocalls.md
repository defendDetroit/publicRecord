### The TCPA Private Right of Action

You do NOT need a lawyer to sue a robocaller under the TCPA. You can:

1. Log every call (date, time, number, content)
2. File in **small claims court** in your county
3. Cite **47 U.S.C. §227(b)(3)** — private right of action
4. Request **$500 per call** ($1,500 if willful)
5. The burden of proof is on THEM to show they had consent

People have won thousands of dollars in small claims court against robocallers. The statute exists for exactly this purpose.
