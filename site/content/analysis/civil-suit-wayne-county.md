**Plaintiff:** Kevin Mok, pro se  
**Defendants:** Brandon Banks; 36th District Court (Wayne County); Does 1-10

## I. NATURE OF THE ACTION

This action arises from the filing of a fraudulent ex parte Personal Protection Order (PPO) containing materially false statements made under oath, the subsequent entry of that PPO into the Law Enforcement Information Network (LEIN) before service upon the respondent, and the cascading defamatory consequences of that entry across every system that queries LEIN — including but not limited to housing, employment, banking, and law enforcement databases.

The information asymmetry created by the ex parte process — wherein the state broadcasts accusations before the accused is notified — constitutes a systemic due process violation that this action seeks to remedy both individually and as a matter of public concern.

## III. FACTUAL BACKGROUND

### A. The Ex Parte Filing

On or about the date of filing, Defendant Banks submitted a petition for a Personal Protection Order under MCL 600.2950, making statements under oath. These statements contained material misrepresentations of fact, constituting perjury under MCL 750.423.

### B. LEIN Entry Before Service

Upon the ex parte grant, the PPO was entered into the Law Enforcement Information Network (LEIN) — Michigan's statewide law enforcement database — **before Plaintiff was served** with notice of the PPO's existence. This created an information asymmetry wherein:

1. Every law enforcement officer in Michigan could see the PPO
2. Every background check system querying LEIN could see the PPO
3. The Plaintiff could not see, challenge, or respond to the PPO
4. The Plaintiff did not know the PPO existed

### C. The Cascade: Life Freeze

The LEIN entry triggered cascading consequences across every system that performs background checks:

| System | Impact | Due Process |
|--------|--------|-------------|
| **Law enforcement** | Plaintiff flagged in every police interaction | None — entry before service |
| **Housing/rental** | Background check returns PPO | None — no notice given |
| **Employment** | Background check returns PPO | None — no opportunity to explain |
| **Banking** | Risk assessment affected | None — no notification |
| **Professional licensing** | May trigger review | None — no hearing |

This cascade constitutes a **life freeze** — the functional equivalent of a civil death sentence imposed without notice, hearing, or opportunity to respond.

### D. The Information Asymmetry Chain

```
Banks files ex parte PPO (cost: $25)
        ↓
Court grants without hearing (ex parte)
        ↓
PPO enters LEIN (BEFORE service)
        ↓
Every system in Michigan sees PPO
        ↓

        ↓

```

**The cost to file: $25.

## IV. CLAIMS

### Count I: Perjury (MCL 750.423)

Defendant Banks made false statements of material fact in a sworn petition submitted to the 36th District Court. Under Michigan law, any person who makes a false statement under oath in a judicial proceeding commits perjury, a felony punishable by up to 15 years imprisonment.

The false statements include but are not limited to statements regarding threats, contact, and circumstances that are contradicted by:
- Publicly available video evidence (self-witness depositions)
- Documentary evidence in the public record
- Contradictions from the Defendant's own household

### Count II: Defamation Per Se

The filing of a PPO containing false statements, and its subsequent publication through LEIN to every law enforcement and background check system in the state, constitutes defamation per se under Michigan law.

A statement is defamatory per se when it:
1. **Imputes a crime** — A PPO implies the respondent engaged in threatening or harassing conduct
2. **Injures professional reputation** — LEIN entry affects employment background checks
3. **Tends to injure in business** — Banking, housing, and professional systems query LEIN

The LEIN entry is **publication** — it is broadcast to thousands of systems and agencies. The statements in the PPO are the **defamatory content**. The ex parte nature means the Plaintiff had **no opportunity to prevent publication**.

### Count III: Abuse of Process

Defendant Banks used the PPO process not for its intended purpose of protection, but as a weapon to:
- Create an information asymmetry favoring the filer
- Impose consequences on the Plaintiff without due process
- Leverage the LEIN system as a tool of harassment
- Freeze the Plaintiff out of housing, employment, and banking

The PPO process was designed to protect victims. When used fraudulently, it becomes the instrument of abuse it was meant to prevent.

### Count IV: Due Process Violation (42 U.S.C. § 1983)

The 36th District Court, as a state actor, entered the PPO into LEIN before service, depriving the Plaintiff of liberty and property interests without due process of law in violation of the Fourteenth Amendment.

The ex parte → LEIN → life freeze chain creates a deprivation of constitutionally protected interests:
- **Liberty:** Freedom from false criminal-adjacent records
- **Property:** Employment, housing, banking access
- **Reputation:** Publication to every system querying LEIN

### Count V: Defamation by Omission / Institutional Failure

The 36th District Court failed to:
1. Verify the truthfulness of the ex parte petition
2. Provide notice before LEIN entry
3. Establish any mechanism for pre-broadcast challenge
4. Limit the scope of LEIN publication to necessary parties

This systemic failure constitutes deliberate indifference to the known risk that false PPO filings impose cascading harm on innocent respondents.
