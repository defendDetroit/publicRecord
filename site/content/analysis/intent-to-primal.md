## Why .primal

The word *primal* means:

1. **First in time.** Original. Fundamental. The thing before the thing.
2. **Of or relating to an early stage.** The seed. The origin. The root.
3. **Essential.** Not derived. Not secondary. Not dependent.

The ecoPrimals architecture is built on primal computing \u2014 sovereign infrastructure, consent-based data, biological metaphors for digital systems. The membrane. The immune system. The primals (neural, barracuda, beardog, songbird, dag, squirrel...).

`.primal` is the namespace this architecture was built for:

```
membrane.primal
detroit.primal
nestgate.primal
thesis.primal
water.primal
```
