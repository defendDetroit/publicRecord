## The Chain Forms at Three

With one pulse point: no chain. Just a beat.

With two pulse points: a chain of length 1. Binary. The signal bounces between them. Predictable.

With three pulse points: the chain folds into a loop. The signal goes A→B→C→A, but the return path A→C→B→A has a *different delay*. The asymmetry between the two directions IS the remainder. The chain depth is no longer zero — it's the number of times the signal has circumnavigated the triangle.

```

One-way probability:                      0.00175

```

The signal goes out easily. It almost never comes back the same way. That's because the triangle is *chiral* — it has a handedness. A→B→C is not the same as A→C→B. The irrational center shifts depending on which direction you traverse. And that shift IS the identity.
