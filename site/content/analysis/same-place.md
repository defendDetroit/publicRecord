## How It Works

### Step 1: Legalize the Product

Sports betting was illegal in Michigan until December 2019. DraftKings, FanDuel, and MGM **lobbied the Michigan legislature** to pass HB 4311 (Lawful Sports Betting Act).

The same legislature where **Brian Banks ran for State Senate**. The same legislature where **Lee Chatfield** (Speaker, now facing 13 felonies) controlled the calendar. The same legislature where **Dykema Gossett** operates the largest political compliance operation in the state.

### Step 2: Build the Ad Machine

Once legal, sports betting companies spent **billions** on advertising:

| Company | Annual US Ad Spend | Platform |
|---------|-------------------|----------|
| DraftKings | ~$1B | TV, digital, text, push notifications |
| FanDuel | ~$800M | TV, digital, text, push notifications |
| BetMGM | ~$500M | TV, digital, text, push notifications |

These ads run through **Meta** (Facebook/Instagram), **Google** (YouTube/Search), and **carrier networks** (AT&T, T-Mobile, Verizon) as push notifications and texts.

### Step 3: Use the Same Infrastructure for Politics

The digital ad platforms don't distinguish between:
- A DraftKings ad ("BET THE LIONS")
- A political PAC ad ("VOTE FOR X")
- A robocall from a dark money group

They all use the **same targeting data**, the **same delivery networks**, and the **same billing infrastructure**.

| Feature | Sports Betting Ad | Political PAC Ad | Robocall |
|---------|------------------|------------------|----------|
| Targeting | Location + behavior | Location + voter file | Phone number + area code |
| Delivery | Meta/Google/carrier | Meta/Google/carrier | VoIP/carrier |
| Billing | Corporate treasury | PAC/dark money | Untraceable prepaid |
| Consent | Buried in app TOS | None required (political exemption) | None (illegal but unenforced) |
| Opt-out | Difficult | Impossible | Impossible |
