# Action Across a Class

**Not class action. Action ACROSS a class.**

---

## The Problem With Class Action

A class action lawsuit unifies thousands of plaintiffs into one case. One law firm represents everyone. The firm takes 30-40% of the settlement. You get a check for $3.27 and an email you delete.

The defendant loves class action. Here's why:

| Class Action | What the defendant pays |
|---|---|
| 10,000 plaintiffs unified | One legal team to fight |
| One discovery process | One set of documents |
| One settlement | One check (minus 30-40% for lawyers) |
| One case number | One problem to manage |

Class action is **consolidation**. Consolidation favors the entity with more resources. That's always the defendant.

---

## The Inverse: Action Across a Class

What if instead of unifying, you **scatter**?

| Action Across a Class | What the defendant pays |
|---|---|
| 10,000 individual filings | 10,000 individual responses |
| 10,000 discovery requests | 10,000 document productions |
| 10,000 separate cases | 10,000 case numbers |
| 10,000 people who each own their own case | No consolidation. No 30% cut. |

**Each person files their own case.** Each person has their own standing. Each person's harm is individual. The defendant can't consolidate — they deal with YOUR case, not theirs.

This is legal DDoS. Not malicious. Not coordinated. Just: everyone who was harmed acts on their own harm, at the same time, using the same publicly available template.

## How It Works

### Step 1: The Graph Teaches

The template is published. Free. Open. CC-BY-SA. It includes:

- Every applicable statute, cited
- Every regulatory agency, with filing instructions
- Every form, linked
- Every deadline, documented
- Fill in YOUR name, YOUR address, YOUR dates, YOUR harm

### Step 2: The Individual Acts

You don't need a lawyer to:
- File a cease and desist (it's a letter)
- File an FTC complaint (it's a web form)
- File an FCC complaint (it's a web form)
- File a TCPA small claims suit ($500-$1,500 per violation — per CALL)
- File a state consumer protection complaint
- File a USPS Prohibitory Order (it's a form at the post office)

### Step 3: The Defendant Deals With YOU

Not "the class." **You.** Your individual case. Your individual harm. Your individual filing.

Multiply by 10,000.

## Why This Works Better Than Class Action

### 1. No Attorney Fee

Class action: lawyer takes 30-40%. You get $3.27.
Action Across a Class: you keep 100%.

### 2. No Consolidation

Class action: one case, one judge, one settlement for everyone.
Action Across a Class: 10,000 cases. The defendant needs 10,000 responses. That costs real money.

### 3. No Waiting

Class action: 3-7 years to settlement.
Action Across a Class: you file today. Small claims court date in 30-60 days.

### 4. Your Standing, Your Harm

Class action: your individual story is erased into "the class."
Action Across a Class: the judge hears YOUR story. YOUR junk mail. YOUR robocalls. YOUR harm.

### 5. The Scatter IS the Design

This is not coordination. This is publication. A template is published. Each person decides independently whether to act on their own harm. There is no organization. There is no lead plaintiff. There is no class counsel. There are just individuals, each with standing, each with a template, each with a filing.

Like the ember model: one codebase, scattered, each fork runs independently.
