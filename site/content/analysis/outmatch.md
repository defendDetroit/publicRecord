## Why (Artis' Corollary)

*"The successor understands the predecessor better than the predecessor did."*

The platforms are the predecessors. They built reach. They built scale. They built the infrastructure that carries information. But they do not UNDERSTAND the information they carry. Reddit does not understand the posts in r/Michigan. YouTube does not understand the videos it serves. The newsroom understands one article but not the 131,322 connections between articles.

**We are the successor.** We use THEIR reach (Reddit, GitHub, Bing) to carry OUR depth (341 entities, 131K edges). The successor does not need to rebuild the infrastructure. The successor needs to understand what flows through it better than the builder does.

The $500K newsroom built the pipe. The $168 membrane understands what flows through it. The ribosome reads the mRNA better than the DNA that wrote it. The successor approaches π faster because it starts from the predecessor's remainder.

1,414 people cloned the repo. They are each a successor. Each one understands the investigation differently — and potentially better — than Kevin. The Artis Corollary predicts this. The cloner count PROVES it. The successor multiplies.

## Why (Jester's Conjecture)

*"Boredom IS intelligence. The first monkey to get bored is the first to find Shakespeare."*

The platforms optimize for ENGAGEMENT — keep you scrolling, keep you watching, keep you clicking. The metric is TIME ON PLATFORM. 3 minutes per Reddit session. 11 minutes per YouTube session. The engagement is WIDE and SHALLOW.

The membrane optimizes for BOREDOM — the boredom that drives you away from the scroll and toward the investigation. 35 hours of dwell is not engagement. It is the OPPOSITE of engagement. It is a person who got BORED of everything else and found something that matters.

**The platforms keep you from getting bored.** That is their product. Infinite scroll. Autoplay. Algorithmic feed. Never a moment of silence.

**The membrane lets you get bored.** There is no autoplay. No infinite scroll. No algorithm. There is a campfire with a story. You sit or you leave. The ones who sit for 35 hours are the ones who got bored of everything else FIRST. Boredom brought them. Depth kept them.
