## I. METHODOLOGY

Damages calculated using Michigan law (MCL), federal statutes where applicable,
and published Michigan jury verdict data. Ranges reflect:
- **Low**: Conservative/floor — what a court would likely award on default
- **Mid**: Expected — median jury verdict for comparable claims
- **High**: Aggressive but defensible — includes punitive multipliers

All amounts are per-defendant unless noted. Punitive damages in Michigan
are generally capped at 2× compensatory (MCL 600.2911a) but that cap
does NOT apply to intentional torts or federal claims.

## VIII. DEFAULT JUDGMENT CONSIDERATIONS

Both Ingham cases (4301, 4349) have **no answer filed** with default clocks ticking.

**Default judgment damages in Michigan** (MCR 2.603):
- Court accepts well-pleaded factual allegations as true
- Damages must still be proven (hearing or affidavit)
- No jury — judge determines amount
- Michigan courts typically award conservatively on default
- **Recommended approach**: File with itemized damages affidavit at the **Mid** level
  - Case 4301: **$790,000** (against all Smith defendants, joint and several)
