## THE BIGGER PICTURE — CHARTERS WITH NO OVERSIGHT

The user is right: this is bigger than Banks. The system was already broken.

### Why Charters Are Vulnerable

| Feature | Traditional District | Charter (PSA) |
|---------|---------------------|---------------|
| Hiring oversight | HR department, union, central office | Board (often hand-picked by operator) |
| Credential verification | ISD auditors + MDE compliance | Self-reported to CEPI via REP |
| Financial controls | Elected school board, public budget process | Appointed board, management agreement |
| Teacher pipeline | District recruiting, union scale | Operator hires whoever is cheapest |
| Accountability | Voters, union, superintendent, state | Authorizer (3% fee = incentive to authorize) |

### The DPSCD Authorizer Conflict

DPSCD collects **3% of state aid** (~$147K/year from MacDowell alone) as an authorization fee. Every charter they authorize generates revenue for the district. They authorized PCA under the same operator whose existing school is in the bottom 3%.

**The authorizer has a financial incentive to authorize, not to investigate.**

### The Auditor Problem — Alan C. Young & Associates

Same auditor for **9 consecutive years** (FY2017–FY2025). Every year, clean opinion. Every year, the school slides deeper. The FY2023 O&M overrun (3× budget) got a compliance finding but no referral. The $667K school admin line item never triggered a question about who was being paid that much to run a school with 3% math proficiency.
