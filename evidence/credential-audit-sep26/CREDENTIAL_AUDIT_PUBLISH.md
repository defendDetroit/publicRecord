## What This Means for the Children

MacDowell serves **362 students** — 99.2% Black, 92% economically disadvantaged.

- **Kindergarteners** are being taught by a teacher whose only endorsement is secondary English and Journalism (grades 6-12). She has never been endorsed to teach elementary school.
- **3rd graders** are being taught by someone whose substitute permit was issued to a completely different school.
- **5th graders** — same situation: sub permit from Chandler Park Academy in Harper Woods.
- **8th graders** are being taught by a substitute permit holder as their full-time teacher.
- One **kindergarten teacher** has no credential found in the state system at all.
- One **Dean who also teaches 8th grade** cannot be found in MOECS.

The school reports **3% math proficiency** and **12% reading proficiency** (U.S. News). It ranks in the **bottom 3% of all Michigan schools** (SchoolDigger). Now we know why.

## The Law

**MCL 388.1763(1):** A district shall not employ an individual who is not appropriately placed under a valid certificate.

**MCL 388.1763(2):** 50% state aid deduction for salary paid during violation period.

**MCL 388.1763(6):** If a school official is **notified** by MDE and **knowingly continues** to employ → **misdemeanor**, $1,500 fine per incident.

MDE notified MacDowell's Board President (Nicole Wells-Stallworth) about credential violations on **February 22, 2022**. OEE Analyst Katie Schmiedeknecht told the school on **February 28, 2022** that "there will be no need for an investigation" — then presented the same enforcement rules at 4 state conferences between 2024 and 2026.
