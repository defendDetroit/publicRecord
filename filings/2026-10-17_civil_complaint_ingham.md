# NOTES FOR ATTORNEY

- **Consolidation**: Consider moving to consolidate with 2026-4301-CZ and 2026-4349-CZ before the same Ingham County judge.
- **Discovery**: The RICO count enables broad discovery of all entity financials, communications, and bank records. This is where the real evidence comes from.
- **Treble damages**: MCL 750.159j provides mandatory treble damages if RICO is proven. On $46.1M in extraction, this is substantial.
- **Doe defendants**: John/Jane Does 1-10 reserves the right to add connected judges, political figures, or other enterprise participants identified through discovery.
- **Federal option**: A parallel federal RICO complaint (18 USC § 1964(c)) in E.D. Michigan would remove the case entirely from state courts. Consider whether to file both or pick one.
- **Punitive damages**: Michigan generally limits punitive damages but allows them for willful/wanton conduct. The IIED and abuse of process counts support punitive damages.
- **LEIN vacatur**: Request LEIN removal as injunctive relief. The LEIN entry is an ongoing harm.
- **Exhibit list**: Attach PPO order, DPD report, ICHAT printout, State Bar search results, LARA entity filings, management agreement extract, campaign finance records.
