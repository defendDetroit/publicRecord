## I. CORRECTION: IDENTITY OF THE ACTUAL FORGER

My original complaint identified Marquita L. Smith as the person who
went to the Harper Woods MI post office on August 21, 2026, and signed
for four (4) certified mail pieces addressed to:

1. Shayla Smith (×2)
2. Leon A. Smith
3. Simone Smith

Each return receipt (PS Form 3811) was signed "S Smith" under the
addressee's name.

**I now have evidence that Leon A. Smith — not Marquita L. Smith —
was the actual forger of all four signatures.** Specifically:

### A. Handwriting Comparison

I obtained authentic, notarized signatures for all three named
recipients from Wayne County Register of Deeds records and federal
bankruptcy petitions:

| Person | Source Document | Date | Type |
|--------|---------------|------|------|
| **Leon A. Smith** | Quit Claim Deed, 17370 Dresden St (Wayne ROD Doc# 2025200597) | Jun 24, 2025 | Wet ink, notarized |
| **Leon A. Smith** | Lake Trust CU Mortgage, $68,000 (Wayne ROD Doc# 2025201292) | Jun 24, 2025 | Wet ink, notarized |
| **Marquita L. Smith** | Same Lake Trust mortgage (co-borrower) | Jun 24, 2025 | Wet ink, notarized |
| **Marquita L. Smith** | Warranty Deed, 10344 West Point St (Wayne ROD Doc# 2026190555) | May 27, 2026 | Wet ink, notarized |
| **Simone R. Smith** | Wayne County ROD mortgage documents (multiple) | 2005–2021 | Wet ink, notarized |

**Findings from comparison (see Exhibits B-1 through B-3):**

1. All five green card signatures share the **same flowing "Smith"
   formation** — identical pen pressure, identical "S" tail direction,
   identical "th" ending stroke. This is unmistakably one person
   writing "Smith" five times while pretending to be different people.

2. The Lake Trust Credit Union mortgage (Doc# 2025201292, June 24, 2025)
   is the definitive baseline: **Leon and Marquita both signed the same
   document on the same day before a notary.** Their handwriting is
   visibly and distinctly different:
   - Leon signs with a **flowing, forward-leaning cursive** with
     distinctive looping letter formations.
   - Marquita signs with a **more angular, compact style** with a
     completely different "S" tail direction.

3. The green card signatures **do not match Marquita's** authentic
   writing style in any characteristic — letter formation, slant,
   pressure, or "S" tail direction.

4. The green card signatures **are consistent with Leon's** flowing
   cursive style, forward slant, and distinctive "S" formation.

5. Simone R. Smith's authentic signatures from multiple Wayne County
   ROD mortgage documents (spanning 2005-2021) show a consistent
   personal style that bears **no resemblance** to the green card
   "S Smith" scrawl attributed to her.

6. **Conclusion:** Leon A. Smith, the USPS mail carrier assigned to
   the very facility through which these letters were routed, forged
   the signatures on return receipts for mail addressed to his wife
   (Marquita), his daughter (Shayla ×2), his sister-in-law (Simone),
   and himself — all while employed as a federal postal worker with
   facility access.
